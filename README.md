PixelNOW is a premium, native macOS cloud gaming client designed to give you the best possible GeForce NOW experience on your Mac.

## Features

- **Native macOS Experience:** Built specifically for Mac, offering a fast, fluid, and beautiful interface that feels right at home.
- **Seamless Streaming:** Browse your catalog, launch, and play your favorite PC games instantly via GeForce NOW.
- **Built-in Recording:** Capture your best gaming moments easily with integrated session recording.
- **Advanced Discovery:** Quickly find what to play next with beautifully organized game rails, powerful search, and detailed filters.
